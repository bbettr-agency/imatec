## 1. Current-page audit

The page currently reads as *"homepage sections stacked + generic prose boxes."* Specifics:

- **Hero** — still the **old boxed image-card** (rounded panel, border, corner tick) — "text left + rectangular photo right." Not integrated like the refined homepage hero, and it **duplicates the homepage's roller-bench crop**.
- **"The problem" & "Can your roller be recoated?"** — `TextSection` prose blocks in `max-w-3xl`: eyebrow → heading → paragraph, with the **right ~40% of each section empty** (dead space). The exact generic pattern to remove.
- **Recoat vs Replace, Process, Final CTA** — **verbatim homepage components**, carrying the **homepage's datum indices (02 / 04 / 07)** — out of sequence here, and it feels copied.
- **"How to send us your roller"** — five **rounded-panel cards** (card soup).
- **FAQ** — the refined 2-col accordion (fine, reusable).
- **No enquiry form** — only WhatsApp buttons. A flagship lead-gen page needs a structured recoating enquiry path.
- **No bridge** — nothing explicitly intercepts replacement-intent searchers.

SEO baseline is sound (title, Service + Breadcrumb + FAQPage schema, one H1, alt text) — we keep and extend it.

## 2. Search-intent analysis

From the SEO research, this page must serve **four overlapping intents**, and its defining job is to **bridge** them:

| Intent | What they type | Serve with |
|---|---|---|
| **Recoating (correct term)** | "fuser roller recoating", "hot roller recoating", "fuser roller reconditioning / resurfacing / repair" | the whole page; own the category (no direct SA competitor found) |
| **Replacement (what most type)** | "fuser unit price", "replace fuser roller", "new fuser roller", "[brand] fuser unit" | **the bridge** — intercept and reframe to recoating; link to `/printer-copier-parts` for those who still want a new part |
| **Technician (symptom/component)** | "fuser film tearing", "pressure roller", "roller marking pages", "worn fuser roller" | "what recoating is / what it solves" + "what we work with" (component-level language) |
| **Local + national** | "fuser roller recoating Centurion / Gauteng / South Africa" | Centurion workshop signal + "we recoat for the trade nationwide (courier your roller in)" |

**The bridge is the page's core mechanic:** *"Looking to replace a worn fuser/hot roller? Before you buy — and import — a new part, recoating may restore the one you have."* Terminology: lead with **"fuser roller"** (public), keep **"hot roller"** secondary; use "fuser unit", "pressure roller", "reconditioning", "repair", "replacement" naturally — no stuffing.

## 3. Conversion journey

`SEARCH (worn roller / need a new fuser)` → **land here** → *"right place — they specialise in exactly this"* → *"what recoating even is"* → *"why recoat instead of replace"* → *"can they help MY roller?"* → *"can I trust them with the part?"* → *"what happens when I send it"* → *"what do I send / how fast can I talk to someone"* → **WhatsApp / enquiry**. Each section removes one doubt; CTAs sit at the decision points, not every screen.

## 4. Proposed final section order

Hero, then eight numbered movements (page-specific datum indices — **not** the homepage's):

0. **Hero** — intent-matched, bridge-aware, integrated roller imagery, clear CTA.
1. **What recoating is** (+ the symptoms it solves) — plain language; catches non-experts and symptom-searchers.
2. **Recoat vs replace** — the bridge/decision (deeper than the homepage; page-specific composition).
3. **When recoating makes sense — and when it doesn't** — candidacy; honesty builds trust and answers "can you help *my* roller?".
4. **Why IMATEC** — specialist credibility (30+ yrs 🟡, own workshop, bench-tested, we vet).
5. **How it works** — assessment → recoat → bench-test → return (page-specific worksheet).
6. **What we recoat & what to send us** — component types + brands + the practical ask (reduces friction into the CTA).
7. **FAQ** — objection handling (expanded, all truthful).
8. **Enquiry** — WhatsApp (pre-filled) **+ a structured recoating form** — the conversion close.

*(I merged the brief's "supported brands" and "what to send" into §6 for tightness; happy to split if you prefer.)*

## 5. Section-by-section visual composition

Shared language with the homepage (datum `SectionMark`, big Open Sans, decision typography, worksheet, dissolved imagery) — but **page-specific content, composition and numbering**. No card soup, no eyebrow→para→3-cards, images integrated not boxed.

- **§1 What recoating is** — a two-part editorial band, **not** a centred prose box. Left: a large plain-language definition ("Recoating restores the worn surface of a fuser or hot roller back to working spec — so the roller keeps running instead of being replaced."). Right: a compact **symptom list** in the trade's own words (marking / streaking / poor fusing / worn or damaged surface) as a clean ruled list — this is where symptom-searchers self-recognise. Fills the canvas; no dead right-half.
- **§2 Recoat vs replace** — echoes the homepage decision *language* but a **distinct, deeper composition**: a full-width "what you're actually deciding" band — *Replace* (buy new · often imported · wait · scrap the core) vs *Recoat* (restore to spec · keep your OEM core · cost-effective alternative), with the honest "we'll quote yours" note and a contextual link to `/printer-copier-parts` for those who still need a new part. Typographic + a datum rule; no image needed (saves the scarce photography).
- **§3 When recoating makes sense** — an **honest two-column** "good candidate / better replaced" split (e.g. *larger fuser & hot rollers, surface wear* vs *film-sleeve machines, damage beyond the surface*) — ⚠️ exact model/brand specifics pending; structured so we drop them in. Honesty here is a conversion asset. Type-led.
- **§4 Why IMATEC** — the homepage's vetting-model *idea*, re-composed for this page around **the roller specifically**: three proof pillars (specialist recoating, our own workshop + bench-test, 30+ years 🟡) on a datum, optionally with a supporting workshop image **bleeding in** (integrated, different crop from the hero) — or type-led if we're conserving the one photo.
- **§5 How it works** — a **bench-worksheet** (big numerals on a datum rule, like the homepage process but page-specific: Assess → Strip & prepare → Recoat → Cure → Bench-test → Return), merged with the old "send us your roller" flow so the visitor sees **one clear path from enquiry to returned roller**. No cards. ⚠️ exact steps/turnaround pending.
- **§6 What we recoat & what to send** — left: component coverage (**fuser rollers · hot rollers · pressure rollers**, printers/copiers/MFPs) + brand context (link the brand list, don't repeat the homepage rail); right: a tight **"what to send us" checklist** (brand · model · what's happening · a photo) that flows straight into the enquiry. This is the practical, friction-reducing pre-CTA moment.
- **§7 FAQ** — the refined 2-col accordion, **expanded** to the real objection set (below), FAQPage schema.
- **§8 Enquiry** — a genuine conversion close: a **structured recoating enquiry form** (brand · model · component · what's happening · optional photo — the existing `EnquiryForm variant="recoating"`) beside the WhatsApp/call path, on a light closing band with a datum. Not a rounded box floating in white.
